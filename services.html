Our Services

From regular checkups to grooming and preventive healthcare, Sony Petcare & Veterinary Clinic provides caring services for your beloved pets.

What We Offer

Complete Care For Your Best Friend

We provide a range of veterinary and pet care services designed to keep your pets healthy, happy and comfortable throughout their lives.

01

General Checkup

Regular health examinations help detect health problems early and keep your pet healthy.

  • Complete physical examination
  • Weight and health monitoring
  • Health advice for pet owners
02

Vaccination

Protect your pets from common infectious diseases with appropriate vaccination schedules.

  • Puppy and kitten vaccination
  • Booster vaccinations
  • Vaccination guidance
03

Pet Grooming

Professional grooming helps your pet stay clean, comfortable and looking their best.

  • Bathing and cleaning
  • Hair trimming
  • Nail trimming
04

Dental Care

Good oral hygiene is an important part of your pet's overall health and comfort.

  • Oral health examination
  • Dental cleaning guidance
  • Dental care advice
05

Preventive Healthcare

Preventive care helps identify potential health concerns before they become serious.

  • Health monitoring
  • Parasite prevention guidance
  • Nutrition advice
06

Emergency Consultation

Support for urgent pet health concerns and guidance on appropriate next steps.

  • Urgent health assessment
  • First-response guidance
  • Referral when required
